# Hi, I'm Soren

I work on applied data science for climate and environmental questions. I’m especially interested in turning complex climate and financial information into analysis that is reproducible, explainable, and useful for decisions.

## Areas I work on

- Climate adaptation finance and corporate climate disclosures
- Climate risk and environmental data analysis
- NLP and machine learning for climate research
- Reproducible workflows, notebooks, and interactive dashboards

## Selected projects

These public repositories are forks of Climate Change AI tutorials. I use them to work through and document practical analysis, notebook, and reproducibility improvements. The original tutorial authors and project credits are retained in each repository.

- [Hurricane wind value-at-risk](https://github.com/SorenCV/hurricane-wind-var) — an ensemble-based workflow for estimating county-level hurricane wind losses.
- [NLP for climate policy analysis](https://github.com/SorenCV/nlp-policy-analysis) — tutorial workflows for classifying climate policy documents with NLP and language models.
- [Coal power monitoring](https://github.com/SorenCV/coal-power-mrv) — a computer vision workflow for estimating coal plant operation from satellite imagery.
- [Climate projection downscaling](https://github.com/SorenCV/downscaling-climate-projections) — a deep-learning tutorial for producing higher-resolution climate projections.
